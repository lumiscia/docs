## Is Lumiscia ready for production?

Lumiscia is in beta. You can build real integrations on it, but pricing, SDKs, render concurrency, limits, and operational behavior may change before general availability. Read the [beta notes](../beta-notes) before you depend on it for production workloads.

## What is a composition?

A composition is a JSON document that describes a video: nodes such as `media_in`, `text`, `shape`, `merge`, and `wgsl_shader`, the connections between them, render settings, and a timeline. The SDK's `Composition` class builds one with types, or you can write the JSON by hand. See [Composition format](../composition-format) and the [node reference](../nodes).

## How does pricing work?

You pay $0.01 for each successful render plus $0.0075 per second of render time, the GPU time spent rendering, after your plan's monthly credit. On Pro the per-second rate is $0.005. Failed renders are free, and time spent queued or waiting for a worker to start is not billed.

The Free plan includes $5 of credit a month and Pro includes $50. When the credit runs out, renders draw from your workspace balance, which you top up from $10. See [Pricing](../pricing).

## How do I get media into a render?

Upload a file or register a URL with the media endpoints first. Then map an alias to the media id (`lumen:<media_id>`) in the render request's `media` manifest and reference the alias from your composition, or name the upload directly as `asset:<id>`. Uploads take images, video, audio and fonts. Media is probed and validated before a GPU sees it, and temporary media expires after 24 hours. See [Media sources](../guides/media-sources).

## How do I know when a render finishes?

Use any of these:

- Call the SDK's `waitForRender`, which reads the render's progress and listens on its WebSocket between reads.
- Poll `GET https://api.lumiscia.com/v1/renders/:id` until `status` is `succeeded` or `failed`.
- Pass a `webhookUrl` when you create the render. Lumiscia posts `render.succeeded` or `render.failed` to it. See [Webhooks](../guides/webhooks).

## Can I test without paying for GPU time?

Yes. Renders created with a test API key skip the GPU and media validation and complete after about 10 seconds with a sample artifact, so you can wire up your integration for free. Uploads made with a test key are free too, and aren't stored. Successful test renders send no webhook, and a test render that fails during submission can send `render.failed`. Each test key runs up to 5 at a time.

To check a composition without rendering it at all, use [`POST /v1/renders/check`](../how-it-works#check-before-you-render).

## Can I preview compositions in the browser?

Yes. `@lumiscia/lumen-react` and `@lumiscia/lumen-svelte` render previews on the viewer's device with WebAssembly. Previews are free and need no API key. You only pay when you submit a render to the API. See [React](../sdks/react) or [Svelte](../sdks/svelte).

## What can Lumiscia render?

An MP4 of the whole timeline, 8 bits per channel, in `h264`, `hevc` or `av1`, from any Lumen 0.6 composition, 3D nodes included. Alpha, stills, image sequences, frame ranges, 10-bit output, ProRes and VP9 are rejected before the render is queued or charged. Models, environment maps and LUT files can't be uploaded, so `model_3d`, `render_3d` environment maps and `color_grade` LUTs can't render on Lumiscia. See [Output](../how-it-works#output).

## What are the render limits?

Every render must fit within:

- 3840 pixels wide and 2160 pixels tall,
- 60 fps,
- 10,800 frames,
- 250 nodes,
- 1 MB of composition JSON,
- 50 manifest entries, and 50 distinct media files counting `asset:<id>` references.

Until a workspace tops up or subscribes to Pro, renders are also limited to the pixel count of 1920×1080 and to 120 seconds of output, and artifacts are kept for 1 day. After that, artifacts are kept for 7 days. Uploaded media files can be up to 1 GiB, and fonts up to 20 MiB, but a file a render uses must be at most 512 MiB, or the render fails with `media_download_too_large`.

## Why is my render queued?

Each workspace runs a limited number of renders at a time: 1 before its first top-up, 3 after it, and 10 on Pro or after $100 in total top-ups. Renders past that limit wait in `queued`, and workspaces that have never topped up run after paid work. A [batch render](../pricing#batch-renders) waits for idle GPU time, and after 12 hours it is queued like a standard render. Queue time also grows when workers are cold or capacity is changing. Queue time is not billed. See [queue priority](../pricing#queue-priority).

## Can I run Lumen myself?

Yes. Lumen, the engine behind Lumiscia, is open source under Apache 2.0. `lumen-server` gives you a local render service, but queueing, storage, scaling, and monitoring are up to you. See [Self-hosting](../open-source/self-hosting).

## Where do I report issues?

See [Support](support). Include render ids, input hashes, error codes and request ids, SDK versions, composition JSON, and media details where they apply.
