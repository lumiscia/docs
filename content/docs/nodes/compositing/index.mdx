---
title: Compositing
description: Nodes that combine several rasters into one.
---

Compositing nodes combine several rasters into one. Use them to stack layers, mask them, cut between inputs, and play composition templates on the timeline.

- [Boolean](boolean) (`boolean`): combines two raster alpha masks with boolean operations.
- [Clip](clip) (`clip`): plays a composition template, or its source, over a window of time.
- [Merge](merge) (`merge`): composites an overlay raster over a base raster.
- [Raster Multi Merge](raster-multimerge) (`raster_multimerge`): composites a variadic stack of raster layers in order.
- [Switch](switch) (`switch`): selects one raster input according to a controlled layer index.
