---
title: Processing
description: Nodes that adjust, move and retime a raster.
---

Processing nodes take a raster on `source` and return a raster on `output`. Use them for color, blur, light, geometry, timing and custom shader effects.

- [Blur](blur) (`blur`): applies a separable Gaussian blur to a raster.
- [Color Grade](color-grade) (`color_grade`): applies a LUT-driven color transform to a raster.
- [Crop](crop) (`crop`): keeps a raster region in place and clears everything outside it.
- [Directional Blur](directional-blur) (`directional_blur`): blurs a raster along a line, as a straight motion blur.
- [Displacement Map](displacement-map) (`displacement_map`): moves each pixel of a raster by the pixel of a map raster over it.
- [Exposure](exposure) (`exposure`): adjusts raster exposure, contrast, and offset.
- [Glow](glow) (`glow`): adds a blurred copy of a raster's brightest pixels to it, as light.
- [Hue Saturation](hue-saturation) (`hue_saturation`): adjusts raster hue, saturation, and lightness.
- [Levels](levels) (`levels`): remaps raster black, white, gamma, and output range.
- [Motion Blur](motion-blur) (`motion_blur`): blurs everything that moves in its source by rendering it at several sub-frame times.
- [Opacity](opacity) (`opacity`): uniformly adjusts raster opacity.
- [Radial Blur](radial-blur) (`radial_blur`): blurs a raster along circles about a centre, as a spin.
- [Resize](resize) (`resize`): resamples a raster into a rectangle placed on the composition canvas.
- [Shadow](shadow) (`shadow`): composites a blurred alpha shadow behind a raster.
- [Time Remap](time-remap) (`time_remap`): evaluates a raster input at another frame.
- [Transform](transform) (`transform`): transforms a raster into the composition canvas bounds.
- [WGSL Shader](wgsl-shader) (`wgsl_shader`): runs a custom WGSL compute shader over one or more rasters.
- [Zoom Blur](zoom-blur) (`zoom_blur`): blurs a raster along rays from a centre, as a zoom.
