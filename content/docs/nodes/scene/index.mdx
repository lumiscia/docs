---
title: Scene
description: Nodes that build 3D scenes and draw them into a raster.
---

Scene nodes build a 3D scene of objects, lights and cameras. Every scene node outputs a `scene`, which only scene inputs take, and [`render_3d`](render-3d) draws a scene into a raster that composites like any other layer. The [3D guide](../../guides/3d) builds a scene step by step.

World units are metres. X points right, Y points up, and a camera looks down its own -Z, so +Z points toward the viewer. `position`, `rotation` and `scale` are `[x, y, z]` values, and `rotation` is in degrees, applied as yaw about Y, then pitch about the turned X, then roll about the turned Z.

- [Camera 3D](camera-3d) (`camera_3d`): the viewpoint `render_3d` draws from.
- [Image Plane 3D](image-plane-3d) (`image_plane_3d`): shows a raster, such as text or video, on a flat rectangle.
- [Instance 3D](instance-3d) (`instance_3d`): draws its input scene many times, each copy placed by expressions.
- [Light 3D](light-3d) (`light_3d`): a directional, point, spot or ambient light.
- [Merge 3D](merge-3d) (`merge_3d`): groups scenes into one, placed together by its transform.
- [Model 3D](model-3d) (`model_3d`): a glTF model with its materials and animation clips.
- [Render 3D](render-3d) (`render_3d`): draws a scene into a raster, or its depth or normals.
- [Shape 3D](shape-3d) (`shape_3d`): a box, sphere, plane, cylinder, torus or cone.
- [Text 3D](text-3d) (`text_3d`): text extruded into a solid.
