`light_3d` lights the scene it is wired into. `light` picks the kind:

| `light`       | Shines                                                                                                                      |
| ------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `directional` | In parallel along the light's -Z, like the sun. Its `position` has no effect.                                               |
| `point`       | Every way from its `position`, out to `range` (0 is unlimited).                                                             |
| `spot`        | In a cone along the light's -Z from its `position`. `spot_angle` is the cone's full angle, and `spot_softness` the fraction of it, from the edge, that fades out. |
| `ambient`     | Evenly on every surface from all around. Its `position` and `rotation` have no effect.                                      |

`intensity` is a unitless multiplier, and point and spot lights fall off with the square of the distance. The default `rotation`, `[-45, 30, 0]`, points a directional or spot light 45° down.

A scene with no lights gets a default key light and ambient fill. [`render_3d`](render-3d) also lights every surface from all around with its `environment`, a built-in studio unless it sets an `environment_map`.

## Shadows

Turn on `cast_shadows` on a directional, spot or point light, and objects between it and others block its light. `shadow_softness` is how wide shadow edges blur, in world units. A directional light's shadows cover what the camera sees out to `shadow_distance`, measured from the camera along its view and fading out over its last tenth, so a larger distance trades sharpness for reach. A spot light's shadows cover its cone, up to 170° across, and a point light's reach every direction.

A `render_3d` draws shadows for the first 8 lights that cast them, in the order its scene inputs are wired. The rest light the scene without shadows, with a `scene.shadow_limit` warning.

## Example

A low sun that casts soft shadows:

```json
{
  "id": 3,
  "type": "light_3d",
  "params": {
    "light": "directional",
    "intensity": 3,
    "rotation": [-55, -40, 0],
    "cast_shadows": true,
    "shadow_softness": 0.04
  }
}
```
