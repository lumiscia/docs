Lumiscia bills for the seconds a GPU spends rendering your composition. Browser previews run locally and are free. The current plans and rates are also on [lumiscia.com/pricing](https://lumiscia.com/pricing).

## Plans

| Plan | Price         | Monthly credit | Renders at a time | Queue priority  | Max resolution |
| ---- | ------------- | -------------: | ----------------: | --------------- | -------------- |
| Free | $0            |             $5 |                 1 | After paid work | 1080p          |
| Pro  | $50 per month |            $50 |                10 | High            | 4K             |

Pro also includes shared workspace billing.

Each month's credit is spent first. When it runs out, renders draw from your workspace balance. You top up the balance in the dashboard, from $10 per top-up.

## Rates

```text
$0.002 per second of render time
5 second minimum per completed render ($0.01)
$0.005 per GB uploaded
```

The GPU worker times each render to the millisecond. Failed renders are never charged. Time spent queued or waiting for a GPU worker to start is not render time, so it never shows up on your bill.

## Queue priority

Renders queue by tier:

- Free workspaces run after paid work.
- Any top-up moves a workspace to standard priority.
- Pro workspaces get high priority. So does any workspace that has spent $100 in total, without a subscription.

## Render time

Render time is the GPU time spent rendering, not the length of the output video. A 30-second clip can take a few seconds or a few dozen, depending on resolution, frame rate, and effects.

The strongest drivers of render time are usually:

- total output frames,
- output resolution,
- source media resolution and decode cost,
- number of video sources needed per frame,
- blur, shadow, shader, and other filter-heavy processing.

Nodes that are not reachable from `media_output` are not evaluated. Remove them from production payloads anyway; they make compositions harder to reason about.

## Estimating cost

The calculator on the pricing page assumes the GPU renders roughly this many frames per second of render time:

| Complexity | Frames per second | Typical graph                     |
| ---------- | ----------------: | --------------------------------- |
| Simple     |               360 | Text, shapes, and images          |
| Standard   |               180 | Video plates, merges, transforms  |
| Heavy      |                60 | Custom shaders, blur, many layers |

These are rough figures. 4K output takes longer, and every real render reports its exact time.

```text
frames = duration_seconds * fps
render_seconds = max(frames / frames_per_second, 5)
cost = render_seconds * 0.002
```

| Scenario                 | Frames | Render time |  Cost |
| ------------------------ | -----: | ----------: | ----: |
| 10 s at 30 fps, simple   |    300 |   5 s (min) | $0.01 |
| 30 s at 30 fps, standard |    900 |         5 s | $0.01 |
| 60 s at 30 fps, heavy    |  1,800 |        30 s | $0.06 |

For example, 400 standard 30-second renders a month use 2,000 seconds of render time, or $4.00, which the Free plan's $5 credit covers.

```ts
function estimateRenderCost({
  durationSeconds,
  fps,
  framesPerSecond,
}: {
  durationSeconds: number;
  fps: number;
  framesPerSecond: number;
}) {
  const frames = durationSeconds * fps;
  const renderSeconds = Math.max(frames / framesPerSecond, 5);

  return { frames, renderSeconds, dollars: renderSeconds * 0.002 };
}

console.log(estimateRenderCost({ durationSeconds: 30, fps: 30, framesPerSecond: 180 }));
// { frames: 900, renderSeconds: 5, dollars: 0.01 }
```

For a closer estimate, render a short representative range and scale its render time by the full frame count.

## API billing behavior

`POST https://api.lumiscia.com/v1/renders` returns `202` with `cached: false` when it creates a render. An identical recent submission from the same workspace returns `200` with `cached: true` and the existing render. Treat both as successful submissions.

The `render` object includes `status`, `inputHash`, and `costMicros` (cost in millionths of a dollar). Render statuses are:

- `queued`
- `processing`
- `succeeded`
- `failed`

Do not rely on deduplication as a permanent cache. Repeated renders outside the deduplication window are billed again.

## Optimization tips

- Render a short representative range before running a full batch.
- Lower the resolution while editing, then switch to final resolution for the production render.
- Avoid high frame rates for content that does not benefit from them.
- Trim video sources with `range_start` and `range_end` when you only need part of a clip.
- Use `memo` when a shared expensive result feeds several downstream nodes.
- Watch for expression changes that produce a new input hash and bypass deduplication.

## FAQ

### Is there a monthly minimum?

No. The Free plan has no monthly fee. You pay only for render time and uploads beyond your monthly credit.

### Is there a per-render minimum?

Yes. Every completed render counts at least 5 seconds of render time ($0.01). After that, render time is measured to the millisecond.

### Are node types priced differently?

No. Heavier nodes take longer to render, which increases render time.

### Do I pay for browser previews?

No. Previews run on your users' devices through WebAssembly. You only pay when you call the render API.

### How do I check what I was charged?

Every charge shows up in the usage history in the dashboard. For questions about a charge, contact [support](help/support) with the render ids involved.
