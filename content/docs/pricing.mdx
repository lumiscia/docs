Lumiscia bills for the time a GPU spends rendering your composition, plus a small fee for media you upload. Browser previews run on the viewer's device and are free. The same plans and rates are on [lumiscia.com/pricing](https://lumiscia.com/pricing).

## Plans

| Plan | Price         | Monthly credit |
| ---- | ------------- | -------------: |
| Free | $0            |             $5 |
| Pro  | $50 per month |            $50 |

Pro also adds high queue priority, 10 renders at a time, 4K output, and shared workspace billing.

Charges come out of the monthly credit first, then your workspace balance. Unused credit expires at the end of the month. You top up the balance in the dashboard, $10 minimum per top-up.

## Rates

| Item        | Rate                                                   |
| ----------- | ------------------------------------------------------ |
| Render time | $0.002 per second, 5 second minimum per render ($0.01) |
| Media       | $0.005 per GB uploaded or imported from a URL          |

The GPU worker measures render time to the millisecond. It counts only rendering, so queue time, worker cold starts, media downloads, and the artifact upload are free. Failed renders cost nothing.

To start a render, your monthly credit and balance together must cover the $0.01 minimum. Otherwise the API rejects the request with a `400`.

## Queue priority

Your workspace tier sets its queue priority, concurrency, and output limits:

| Workspace                     | Queue priority  | Renders at a time | Max resolution | Max length | Artifacts kept |
| ----------------------------- | --------------- | ----------------: | -------------- | ---------- | -------------- |
| Free, never topped up         | After paid work |                 1 | 1080p          | 2 minutes  | 1 day          |
| Any top-up                    | Standard        |                 3 | 4K             | No limit   | 7 days         |
| Pro, or $100 in total top-ups | High            |                10 | 4K             | No limit   | 7 days         |

The resolution limit counts pixels, so 1080p allows 1920×1080 or 1080×1920. Renders past your concurrency limit wait in `queued` until a slot frees up. All tiers share the [API limits](how-it-works#validation), such as 60 fps and 10,800 frames.

## Render time

Render time is GPU time, not the length of the output video. A 30-second clip can take a few seconds or a few dozen, depending on:

- total output frames,
- output resolution,
- source media resolution and decode cost,
- the number of video sources each frame needs,
- blur, shadow, shader, and other filter-heavy nodes.

Nodes that don't connect to `media_output` never render, so they add no render time.

## Estimating cost

The calculator on the pricing page assumes the GPU renders this many frames per second of render time:

| Complexity | Frames per second | Typical graph                     |
| ---------- | ----------------: | --------------------------------- |
| Simple     |               360 | Text, shapes, and images          |
| Standard   |               180 | Video plates, merges, transforms  |
| Heavy      |                60 | Custom shaders, blur, many layers |

These are rough figures. 4K output takes longer, and every render reports its exact cost.

```ts
function estimateRenderCost(durationSeconds: number, fps: number, framesPerSecond: number) {
  const frames = durationSeconds * fps;
  const renderSeconds = Math.max(frames / framesPerSecond, 5);
  return { frames, renderSeconds, dollars: renderSeconds * 0.002 };
}

console.log(estimateRenderCost(30, 30, 180));
// { frames: 900, renderSeconds: 5, dollars: 0.01 }
```

| Scenario                 | Frames | Render time |  Cost |
| ------------------------ | -----: | ----------: | ----: |
| 10 s at 30 fps, simple   |    300 |   5 s (min) | $0.01 |
| 30 s at 30 fps, standard |    900 |         5 s | $0.01 |
| 60 s at 30 fps, heavy    |  1,800 |        30 s | $0.06 |

At these rates, 400 standard 30-second renders use 2,000 seconds of render time ($4.00), which fits in the Free plan's $5 credit. For a closer estimate, render a short representative range and scale its render time by the full frame count.

## Billing in the API

`POST https://api.lumiscia.com/v1/renders` returns `202` with `cached: false` when it creates a render. If your workspace submitted an identical request (same composition, media manifest, and webhook URL) in the last 5 minutes, it returns `200` with `cached: true` and the existing render, and you aren't charged again. Treat both as success. An identical request after 5 minutes creates and bills a new render.

The response's `render` object includes `status` (`queued`, `processing`, `succeeded`, or `failed`), `inputHash`, and `costMicros`, the cost in millionths of a dollar. `costMicros` stays 0 until the render succeeds.

## Lowering cost

- Render a short representative range before a full batch.
- Edit at a lower resolution and render the final version at full size.
- Use the lowest frame rate your content needs.
- Trim video sources with `range_start` and `range_end` on `media_in`.
- Wrap an expensive branch in `memo` so it renders again only when its `key` changes.

## FAQ

### Can I run more than 10 renders at a time?

Yes. After $1,000 in total top-ups, your workspace can run 25 renders at a time with the highest queue priority. Other limits match the Pro row in [Queue priority](#queue-priority). A Pro subscription alone doesn't unlock this tier.

### Is there a monthly minimum?

No. The Free plan has no monthly fee. You pay only for render time and uploads beyond your monthly credit.

### Are node types priced differently?

No. Heavier nodes take longer to render, so they cost more render time.

### Do I pay for browser previews?

No. Previews run on the viewer's device through WebAssembly. You pay only when you call the render API.

### How do I check what I was charged?

Every charge appears in the usage history in the dashboard. For questions about a charge, contact [support](help/support) with the render ids involved.
